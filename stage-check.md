# Stage check

Run this when a company is filed. Run it again on the Friday pass for every company still on pre-seed or seed, and for any later company whose round is missing.

Stage stays closed: pre-seed, seed, or later. Series A and anything after it is later. The round field is the series and the date the company published. Pre-seed and seed stay on the list even with no posting, because a small team can take freelance.

A model does not write the stage. Same rule as the band.

## Where to read

In this order. Stop at the first page that names a round.

1. The company’s own post. A blog, a newsroom note, or a careers page that names the round. The tldraw design engineer posting said Series A. The filed round was still the 2022 seed.
2. A press release the company issued.

A database, a funding tracker, or a post that is not from the company does not move the stage. If two pages disagree, leave the filed round.

## What to copy

Copy the series and the month they used. `Series D, Nov 2025`.

A dollar amount with no letter does not become Series A. Copy the amount and the date, and leave the stage where the last named series put it. Opal announced $10M in May 2026 and did not name a letter, so it stays on seed.

If the page does not name a round, leave the stage off. Screen Studio stays on the open list. Do not infer a round from headcount, revenue, or a Crunchbase row the company did not publish.
